# Research

Use for a bounded question whose answer can guide a baseline policy or automation contract.

1. State the question, decision owner, and the evidence needed. Prefer official documentation, source code, specifications, and first-party APIs.
2. Refresh sources without mutation. Link every factual claim to its owning source and distinguish observed fact from inference.
3. Write one concise Markdown finding in the established repository location. Include scope, evidence, conclusion, unknowns, and recommended next action.
4. The root Hive may admit a separately authorized, user-visible research work package; that package already satisfies the background-research role and need not delegate recursively. Its accountable worker may use bounded internal source streams or review when they materially improve coverage, while retaining source verification, synthesis, validation, and final reporting.

Never include secrets, raw logs, or private operational data.
